Loading the clouds

Believing I can become anything, so imagination becomes reality

My school record once read: "she wants to do too many things to have just one dream."
There was a time I focused on those words — no dream — and let them worry me.

Looking back, I don't think I ever wanted to be defined by a single label.
Creating doesn't belong to one field. I wanted to be someone unbound by a single mold — someone who could make anything.

Living a finite stretch of time in such a wide world, there are far too many temptations to settle on just one thing.
The world is so interesting and beautiful that I could never stop imagining.

I suppose I'm not someone without a dream, but someone with too many things she wants to do.
I hope that stays true — that I keep finding new things I want to do.

What I pursue

Kindness

I was deeply inspired by Andersen's tale of the Wind and the Sun — it wasn't the fierce wind but the sun's warmth that made the traveler take off his coat. Warmth has the power to open people's hearts and move them to act on their own. I pay close attention to small discomforts and difficulties, and I aim to create and act from the perspective of those who need help most. Like inventions that expand convenience and designs that improve accessibility, I believe solutions rooted in consideration can drive better change. I pursue work and actions infused with warmth that can move this world in a better direction.

Creativity

(Draft) I believe creative ideas are the starting point of innovation. Grounded in constant, careful observation, I enjoy exploring and finding something new even in the smallest details. I question familiar outcomes and pursue change by searching for better directions.

Challenge

(Draft) I strive for an attitude of continually trying new things without ever stopping. I believe failure isn't discouragement, but a moment of inspiration to try again. I pursue a way of enjoying challenges without hesitating in the face of uncertain outcomes.

Infinity

I place no self-imposed limits on possibility, aiming for a mindset that expands the boundaries of thought without limits. I think about sustainable value across the boundaries of nations, cultures, and time. I pursue standards and direction that stay steady even amid a changing environment.
