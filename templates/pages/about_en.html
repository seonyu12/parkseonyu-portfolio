ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Seonyu Park, a creator.

I turn ideas into content and services quickly, then build the brand that fits them. Rather than stopping at profit, my goal is to create work that shares value with people and leaves a positive impact on someone.

Instead of content that provokes stimulation and conflict, I aim for content and services that help people feel a moment of ease and understand each other a little better. To do this, I'm building data analysis skills to understand markets and users, coding and design skills to bring ideas to life, database management skills to run services reliably, and language skills to communicate with more people.
